# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads protect the organization and customers by ensuring that projects meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements before launch.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and privacy requirements for features and integrations
- Assess risk and coordinate remediation planning with engineering
- Confirm compliance gates and controls are in place before release
- Partner with stakeholders to escalate unresolved security issues early
- Maintain documentation for audit and governance needs

### Goals
- Reduce product risk and security exposure
- Support compliant, trustworthy delivery
- Ensure security requirements are addressed before and during release

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and risk assessments
- Escalation notes for vulnerable or blocking issues
- Compliance readiness reviews with PM, product, and leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to review technical design, threat models, and remediation plans
- Partners with Project Managers and Product Managers to align security gates with milestones and release timing
- Escalates issues to stakeholders and leadership when business or compliance risk requires action

---

## Customer Success / Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Adoption Leads represent the customer experience and ensure that product value translates into real adoption, onboarding success, and sustained usage.

### Responsibilities
- Represent user impact and adoption needs throughout delivery
- Validate onboarding, enablement, and customer readiness requirements
- Coordinate feedback loops with support, sales, and customer-facing teams
- Surface product friction, usage risks, and training needs early
- Help translate customer outcomes into delivery priorities

### Goals
- Increase customer confidence and adoption
- Ensure delivery aligns with real user value and experience
- Improve retention and long-term product success

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and usage insights
- Adoption and onboarding readiness reviews
- Cross-functional planning with Product, PM, and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to validate problem framing and success metrics
- Collaborates with Project Managers to ensure adoption milestones and customer communications are coordinated
- Shares customer impact and feedback with Developers so implementation decisions support usability and onboarding

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define how success is measured, monitor project outcomes, and ensure teams make decisions based on evidence from product and operational data.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Monitor adoption, performance, and customer behavior trends
- Support experiment design, data quality validation, and measurement planning
- Translate business questions into measurable indicators for the team
- Partner with stakeholders to interpret results and inform iteration decisions

### Goals
- Make decisions using evidence rather than assumptions
- Improve product performance and outcome visibility
- Support continuous learning and iterative improvement

### Typical Communication
- Dashboard reviews and KPI reporting
- Weekly or milestone metric updates
- Data-informed recommendations for roadmap or release decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine success metrics and business outcomes
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to validate instrumentation, monitoring, and reporting accuracy
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting delivery risks tied to product metrics or adoption trends

---

## Release Manager / QA Lead

### Role Summary
Release Managers and QA Leads coordinate readiness, quality gates, and rollout activities so teams deliver software safely and predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate test readiness and release sequencing across teams
- Maintain deployment checklists and release readiness criteria
- Verify quality gates are met before launch
- Support smoke testing, rollback readiness, and incident preparation
- Partner with stakeholders on launch communication and rollback decisions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and production surprises
- Ensure quality standards are met before deployment
- Improve predictability across the delivery lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Status updates on test coverage, issues, and launch risk
- Coordination with PM, engineering, and stakeholder groups during rollout

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to confirm feature quality and sign-off criteria are met
- Partners with Project Managers to align release windows, dependencies, and communications
- Coordinates with Security and Compliance Leads to ensure release readiness includes risk and control checks

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify ownership, handoffs, and accountability across planning, execution, quality, and release.
