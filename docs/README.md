# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository includes the core project management process documents used by OctoAcme to keep work aligned, transparent, and repeatable across teams and milestones.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle across the full project journey:

- Initiation: validate the business need, confirm stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether to move forward.
- Planning: turn the approved initiative into a backlog, milestones, roles, dependencies, and a release plan.
- Execution and tracking: deliver in small increments, track progress with standups and project boards, and maintain quality through reviews and testing.
- Risk and communication management: identify blockers, dependencies, and escalation paths early and communicate updates to the right audiences.
- Release and deployment: verify readiness, ship safely, and prepare rollback or mitigation steps when needed.
- Retrospective and continuous improvement: capture lessons learned, create action items, and improve the process over time.

## Purpose

These documents provide a shared operating model for how OctoAcme handles project work, from early concept to delivery and learning. They help teams coordinate roles, communicate clearly, manage risk, and execute with consistency.
